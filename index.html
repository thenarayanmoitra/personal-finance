Backups and sync

Automatic backups on this device

A full copy is kept every day you open the app, and again before anything replaces data in bulk: a restore, a statement import, or a sync that would drop entries this device still has. The last 35 daily copies and 40 of the others are kept. Restoring one takes a fresh copy first, so it can be undone too.
